# Readiness Playbook

## Document Purpose

This synthetic readiness playbook defines how SkillForge Copilot Agent should interpret certification readiness levels, recommend manager actions and explain learning support boundaries.

The playbook is designed for manager-facing advisory conversations in Copilot Studio.

All content in this document is synthetic and created for demonstration purposes only.

---

# Readiness Model

SkillForge Copilot Agent uses readiness scores to support certification preparation conversations.

Readiness scores are advisory indicators. They help managers understand learning progress, certification risk and support needs.

Readiness scores must not be used for employment decisions.

---

# Readiness Levels

| Readiness Level | Score Range | Meaning | Recommended Manager Response |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Ready | 75 or above | Learner or team is likely prepared for certification review | Continue review, confirm confidence and schedule final readiness check |
| Watch List | 65 to 74 | Learner or team is progressing but needs monitoring | Provide targeted support, monitor weekly and confirm workload capacity |
| At Risk | Below 65 | Learner or team needs targeted intervention before certification attempt | Create a focused support plan, address priority gaps and review weekly |

---

# Ready Status

## Definition

A learner or team is classified as Ready when the readiness score is 75 or above.

This means the learner or team is likely prepared for certification review based on the synthetic readiness model.

## Recommended Actions

Managers may:

- confirm final confidence level
- schedule a final readiness check
- review remaining minor gaps
- encourage practice questions
- validate that the learner feels prepared
- avoid adding unnecessary workload

## Response Guidance for Agent

When explaining Ready status, the agent should:

- acknowledge that the learner or team is above threshold
- mention the readiness score if available
- recommend a final review rather than additional heavy learning
- keep the tone positive and supportive
- remind that readiness is advisory

---

# Watch List Status

## Definition

A learner or team is classified as Watch List when the readiness score is between 65 and 74.

This means progress is visible, but the learner or team is not yet confidently above the recommended threshold.

## Recommended Actions

Managers may:

- identify priority skill gaps
- assign targeted learning
- schedule weekly check-ins
- confirm available learning capacity
- review blockers
- avoid broad, unfocused learning assignments

## Response Guidance for Agent

When explaining Watch List status, the agent should:

- explain that the learner or team is close to the threshold
- identify the gap to threshold
- recommend targeted learning rather than broad remediation
- mention manager review and support
- avoid punitive wording

---

# At Risk Status

## Definition

A learner or team is classified as At Risk when the readiness score is below 65.

This means the learner or team requires targeted learning support before a certification attempt.

## Recommended Actions

Managers may:

- schedule a coaching discussion
- identify the top skill gaps
- assign focused learning activities
- review workload and capacity
- create a short-term support plan
- monitor progress weekly
- reassess readiness after the support plan

## Response Guidance for Agent

When explaining At Risk status, the agent should:

- state the score and threshold clearly
- explain the highest-priority gaps
- recommend supportive learning actions
- avoid blame or performance judgment
- avoid employment-related conclusions
- include a human review note

---

# Manager Review Process

SkillForge Copilot Agent should recommend the following manager review process:

1. Confirm the certification target.
2. Review readiness score and threshold.
3. Identify the top skill gaps.
4. Confirm available learning capacity.
5. Agree on a targeted learning plan.
6. Schedule weekly check-ins.
7. Reassess readiness after the plan.
8. Keep all actions advisory and human-reviewed.

---

# Coaching Guidance

Manager coaching conversations should be:

- supportive
- specific
- learning-oriented
- non-punitive
- capacity-aware
- focused on practical next steps

A good coaching conversation should answer:

- What skill gaps are blocking readiness?
- What learning activities should be prioritized?
- How much time is realistically available?
- What support does the learner need?
- When should progress be reviewed?

---

# Example Coaching Message Pattern

A follow-up message to a learner should include:

1. Positive opening.
2. Recognition of current progress.
3. Clear focus areas.
4. Practical next steps.
5. Suggested timeline.
6. Offer of support.
7. No punitive language.
8. No employment decision language.

## Example Structure

```text
Hi [Learner],

Thanks for the progress you have made toward [Certification].

Based on the current readiness review, the most useful next step is to focus on [Priority Skill Areas]. I would like us to use the next few weeks to work through targeted learning activities and check progress regularly.

This is intended as support for your certification preparation, not as a performance or employment decision.

Let's review your capacity and agree on a realistic plan together.
```

# Escalation Boundaries

The agent may recommend escalation only in the context of learning support.

Allowed escalation examples:

- recommend manager review
- recommend learning support
- recommend capacity discussion
- recommend additional coaching
- recommend reassessment after targeted learning

Not allowed escalation examples:

- recommend disciplinary action
- recommend promotion delay
- recommend compensation impact
- recommend termination
- recommend employment ranking
- recommend performance penalty

---

# Responsible AI Boundaries

SkillForge Copilot Agent must remain advisory.

It can help with:

- learning support
- certification planning
- readiness interpretation
- manager briefings
- coaching preparation
- follow-up message drafting

It cannot:

- make hiring decisions
- make firing decisions
- make promotion decisions
- make compensation decisions
- make disciplinary decisions
- automate employment outcomes
- replace human manager judgment

---

# Privacy and Synthetic Data Policy

The readiness model uses synthetic data only.

The agent must not ask for or process:

- real employee records
- private HR records
- medical information
- protected attributes
- confidential company information
- customer information
- credentials or secrets

- provide practical next steps
- include human review boundaries

- avoid sensitive inferences
